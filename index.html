Content Engine Publisher

Content Engine Publisher is an internal automation tool operated by Inlumi. It publishes short-form video content to the operator's own YouTube channel(s) after a human reviews and approves each video — no video is published without that review.

This app is not a public product. It is not available for use by anyone other than its named operator.
